# Sound in motion design (silent-first, sound-ready)
The piece must work muted. When sound is on, every sound is tied to something that moves.

## What gets a sound
| Event | Sound |
|---|---|
| A card, chip or message slides in | soft swipe / whoosh |
| A tap or button press | click, short and dry |
| A notification or bubble pops | pop or ding |
| Coins or numbers collecting / counting up | coin chimes, ticks that speed up with the count |
| A bar or progress fills | rising tone that stops when it settles |
| A check mark ticks | light tick |
| Something lands or snaps into place | impact on the settle frame |
| A camera move or wipe | whoosh leading the hit by ~20 ms |
| Logo reveal | short sting, ≤ 3 s |

## Rules
- Max about one cue per second; let quiet moments breathe.
- Style follows the brand's motion words: calm brands get soft, airy sounds; playful brands get bouncy pops; luxury gets almost none.
- Music: the track's BPM is the clock; cuts and hits on the beat. No track: 120 BPM carousels, 128 reels.
- SFX at least 8 dB under any voice; −14 LUFS master; peaks under −1 dBTP.
- Code-built pieces render silent by default: add SFX and music as a separate layer.
- Generated (generate_audio) or licensed only.
